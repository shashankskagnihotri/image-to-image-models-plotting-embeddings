RELEVANT RESEARCH (MOTIVATION, NOT A CLAIM OF IDENTICAL PCA PROTOCOL)
====================================================================

FlowEdit: Inversion-Free Text-Based Editing Using Pre-Trained Flow Models
https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.08629
https://github.com/fallenshock/FlowEdit

Latent Space Editing in Transformer-Based Flow Matching
https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.10825

FlowAlign: Trajectory-Regularized, Inversion-Free Flow-based Image Editing
https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.23145

These works motivate studying latent flow/editing trajectories. The PCA
analysis here is a self-contained observational protocol: it deliberately
does NOT replace the inference pipeline with FlowEdit's edited ODE, add
steering vectors, fit PCA across all states, or treat SD3.5 and IP2P as the
same class of generative model.
